Corridor planning

Anchor Points and charging hubs that make shared trips practical.

Relay Rider can organize commute interest around public, visible pickup locations and EV charging destinations instead of exposing precise home addresses. These locations are planning examples for the Pasadena–Eagle Rock–Glendale corridor and must be reviewed before any program launch.

Anchor Point: a public meeting or pickup location
Charging hub: an EV charging destination or candidate site
