Rule 2202 support

ZEV/EV corridor data infrastructure supporting Rule 2202 AVR reporting.

Relay Rider can help employers and their certified Employee Transportation Coordinators organize zero-emission commute participation data, validate commute records, analyze corridor and EV signals, and prepare review-ready inputs for Rule 2202 workflows.

Important: Relay Rider is a software and data-support tool. It is not the compliance authority, does not certify employer compliance, and does not replace the employer's certified ETC or applicable South Coast AQMD filing requirements.

Research-stage by design.

Relay Rider is developing institution-supported commuter infrastructure with explicit product, safety, privacy, and compliance boundaries.

Does Relay Rider guarantee transportation?

No. Relay Rider is a research-stage coordination and program platform. It does not guarantee a ride, pickup, driver, incentive, or program placement.

Is Relay Rider a Rule 2202 compliance authority?

No. Relay Rider is a zero-emission commute data and workflow tool that can support a certified ETC's Rule 2202 process. Employers and their ETCs remain responsible for applicable compliance determinations, filings, and program requirements.

Are charging rewards guaranteed?

No. Charging and other incentives depend on sponsor funding, eligibility rules, approved redemption methods, and active programs.

Are the map locations active Relay Rider pickup points?

No. They are illustrative planning concepts unless specifically identified as part of an active institutional pilot.

Does the waitlist collect an exact home address?

No. The public waitlist uses general geography such as ZIP code and destination area. Deeper information should only be requested when operationally necessary.
