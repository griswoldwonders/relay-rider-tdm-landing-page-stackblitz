Why Relay Rider

EV-focused mobility software for institutions designing lower-emission commute programs around shared corridors, explainable match previews, and human review.

◎

Battery-electric routes lead

Prioritize verified battery-electric planned routes while retaining eligible hybrids as a secondary pre-pilot pathway.

▣

Planned, repeatable routes

Coordinate around routes participants already intend to travel—not on-demand dispatch or instant pickup.

✓

Governed participation

▥

Measurable outcomes

Sustainability measures

Translate participation into measurable mobility signals.

Relay Rider helps institutions track observed participation and compare modeled environmental outcomes across commuter scenarios. Metrics remain transparent, directional, and tied to stated assumptions.

✓ Estimated VMT change: baseline commute miles minus modeled shared-route and detour miles.
✓ EV / hybrid participation: reported or verified vehicle status within the governed program.
✓ Parking-demand indicator: estimated peak-period spaces affected by participation scenarios.
✓ Estimated CO₂e impact: scenario-based calculations using documented assumptions.
