Sponsored clean commuting

Ride free. Charge your EV. Move cleaner.

Relay Rider helps institutions sponsor shared commutes. Eligible riders may receive free or reduced-cost trips, while participating EV and hybrid drivers can earn charging credits and other approved rewards for trips they already make.

A sponsored clean-commute marketplace.

Relay Rider connects unused EV passenger capacity, commuter access, institutional incentive budgets, charging infrastructure, and measurable transportation outcomes.

R

Riders gain access

Eligible commuters can receive free or reduced-cost shared trips through institution-funded programs.

  • No direct rider-to-driver payment required
  • General corridor and schedule matching
  • Optional deeper commute survey
EV

Drivers earn clean-mobility rewards

EV and hybrid drivers can offer available seats on planned trips and earn approved incentives.

  • Charging credits or reimbursements
  • Preferred parking or charging access
  • Peak-day and corridor bonuses
I

Institutions fund outcomes

Employers, campuses, and agencies can sponsor participation instead of relying on direct passenger fares.

  • Parking-demand reduction
  • Rule 2202 and TDM support
  • Program and sustainability reporting
EV charging redemption

Turn verified participation into charging value.

Relay Rider can administer institution-funded charging rewards through participating charging networks, prepaid charging accounts, reimbursements, or workplace charging programs.

The reward is tied to an approved participation rule—not an on-demand passenger fare. Examples include completing shared commutes, serving a priority corridor, participating on peak parking days, or connecting riders to an Anchor Point.
