Why Relay Rider exists.

Every day, empty vehicle seats travel the same roads while commuters face rising costs, parking fills, and transportation emissions continue. Relay Rider helps institutions activate trips already happening instead of adding more vehicles.

1

Empty capacity

Planned trips often include unused seats traveling along overlapping corridors.

$

High commute costs

Many commuters are caught between driving alone, costly rideshare, and inflexible alternatives.

P

Parking pressure

Employers and campuses absorb the cost of peak parking demand and limited space.

CO₂

Climate impact

Cleaner mobility requires both better vehicles and better use of the trips already on the road.

Cleaner commuting should reward participation

Instead of paying for congestion, reward people for reducing it.

Institutions can sponsor rider access and fund approved incentives for EV and hybrid drivers. Relay Rider coordinates eligibility, verification, redemption, and reporting without requiring a direct rider-to-driver fare.

Built for responsible pilots.

Relay Rider is currently a research-beta platform. Safety, privacy, eligibility, program rules, and institution-approved verification are designed into the pilot model.

Are rides or incentives guaranteed?

No. Availability depends on a participating sponsor, approved corridor, funding, eligibility, and program rules.

Does Relay Rider operate like an on-demand rideshare service?

No. The current concept focuses on planned commute patterns, institutional support, participant review, and corridor-based programs.

How are charging rewards delivered?

Potential methods include charging-network credits, prepaid charging accounts, reimbursement, workplace charging benefits, or sponsor-approved rewards.

What data does the waitlist collect?

The initial waitlist uses general geography and commute-interest information. It does not request a precise home address, driver license, insurance information, or payment details.
